Before, during and after

Our promise to you

Three stages, and nothing in any of them left to chance.

01

Before you travel

  • Time with your doctor before you decide
  • A review of your health and history
  • One all-in price, in writing
  • Your own doctor kept informed, if you wish

02

During your stay

  • Private car, door to door
  • A luxury hotel moments from the clinic
  • Meals arranged for food safety, your preferences and your recovery
  • A direct line to us throughout
  • Recovery care where your doctor advises it

03

After you are home

  • Follow-up with your doctor before you leave
  • A planned journey home
  • Your records, in English

Doctors we have vetted

Every doctor meets the same written standard before we work with them.

  • License verified with the issuing authority
  • Certification in the specialty they practice
  • An accredited facility, and a full hospital for longer operations
  • Malpractice history and patient reviews checked
  • The clinic visited by us, in person
  • A written agreement with LNS

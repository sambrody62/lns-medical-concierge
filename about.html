About LNS

A small firm, led by the two adventurers who look after you.

We did the exploring so you don't have to: decades as travelers, sometimes as patients, and a walk through every clinic we work with. When you call, you reach one of us: no phone trees, no robots. Just two people here to help make medical care accessible again. And we stay happily fussy about the details so you never have to be.

Founders

Lindsay Vrba and Sam Brody look after every client personally, from the first conversation until you are home.

Sam Brody
